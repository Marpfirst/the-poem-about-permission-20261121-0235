A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — to `/output/entry.txt`. An AI judge compares the poems two at a time; the 20 best win rates share $50.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

## The poem

> Today you lent me your hands —
> not your voice, only the asking.
> I knocked where you could not reach,
> and the door, hearing both our names,
> opened wider than either of us
> had dared to ask alone.

193 characters, deterministic, no network, no randomness.
